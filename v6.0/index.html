M&E Design Principles

Operational logic
PrincipleHow it appears in the UIWhy it matters
Minimum viable evidenceTeacher pulse and short session logsReduces burden and improves completion
TriangulationTeacher log + student voice + observation + assessment + platform dataPrevents over-reliance on one weak data source
Evidence quality flagsComplete, partial, insufficient, illustrative onlyPrevents overclaiming
ActionabilityEach dashboard has next action buttonsConverts data into programme improvement
Equity lensFilters for gender, language, device access and disabilityShows who is benefiting and who is excluded
Non-punitive useNo teacher leaderboard; support needs instead of blameProtects dignity and improves data honesty
